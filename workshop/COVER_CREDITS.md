# Friend View cover

The author approved the minimalist eye-and-cards concept for the Steam Workshop cover. The other two generated proposals remain local only.

Generated using the built-in OpenAI imagegen tool, then fitted to 512 × 512 PNG with Universal Modder's asset pipeline for Steam's sub-1-MB preview requirement. No game assets were extracted for this cover.

Prompt:

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: square Steam Workshop cover for the Slay the Spire 2 co-op mod Friend View. Create a bold, elegant minimalist painted emblem: one large friendly luminous green eye hovering over a fan of three fantasy playing cards, their simple illustrations a sword, a shield and a star. Clearly communicate watching a friend's cards, not controlling them. Deep navy parchment background, emerald and warm ivory highlights, restrained gold edging, strong clean silhouette readable as a small thumbnail. Large accurately spelled title "FRIEND VIEW" below the emblem, generous margins. Original illustration, no official game logo, no extra words, no tiny text, no screenshots, no watermark. One single finished cover, not a collage of options.
